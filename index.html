~/about

Both sides of the ticket.

Most developers hand software off and move on. I've lived on both sides of that handoff.

KL swap in your photo →

For the last six years I've built full-stack applications — then worked the tickets, incidents, and 2 a.m. escalations that followed them. That loop made me a strange kind of engineer: one who designs APIs while thinking about the support engineer who'll debug them at 3 a.m., and the user who just needs the thing to work.

The result is software that's easier to deploy, easier to document, and dramatically easier to stand behind. When something breaks, I don't guess — I read logs, reproduce, root-cause, fix, and write down what happened so it never happens the same way twice.
